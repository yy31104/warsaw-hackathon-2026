How the knee is measured

We add the messages ready in RabbitMQ to the pending inbox rows in PostgreSQL. After warm-up, we compare the median count in three consecutive parts of the offer window. Growth in both steps flags that rate. The first flagged rate is the observed knee for that worker count and key shape.
